How I design

Content alone rarely changes performance. These three habits show up in everything above.

Start from the job

I build around the decisions people actually make at work, using their experience as material instead of treating it as a distraction.

Practice early

Learners apply and question ideas while they are still learning them, through cases, scenarios, and structured questions.

Measure against criteria

Objectives, rubrics, and knowledge checks share one set of criteria, so everyone knows what good performance looks like.
